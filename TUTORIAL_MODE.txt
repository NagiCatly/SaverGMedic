CAMPUS MEDIC - TUTORIAL MODE

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the OOP folder in VS Code or a terminal.
3. Compile: javac *.java
4. Run: java Main
5. At the title screen, press 1 for Tutorial Mode.
6. Press 2 or ENTER for the full game.

TUTORIAL GAMEPLAY
- The tutorial uses the SAME campus map and indoor maps as the main game.
- Press G anytime during the tutorial to open or close the on-screen Tutorial Guide.
- The guide pauses gameplay while it is open.
- Use W/A/S/D or the ARROW KEYS to move.
- Hold SHIFT to run.
- Follow the on-screen arrow to the injured patient.
- Stand close to the patient and press E to interact.
- Read the patient's situation and choose with keys 1, 2, or 3.
- If a choice is incorrect, read the feedback and try again.
- After completing the decisions, stand near the patient and press E to treat.
- Rescue one patient to complete the tutorial.
- At the completion screen, press ENTER to start the real game or ESC to return to the title.

REAL GAME
- The starting shift time is 8:00 minutes.
- Each completed level adds 1:30 minutes to the remaining time.

IMPORTANT
Keep the MAPS and CHARACTER folders inside OOP. Do not rename them.
Run java Main from inside OOP so the game can find MAPS and CHARACTER assets.

NARRATOR-LED STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIAL
The narrator appears at the top of the screen during Tutorial Mode and teaches the player as they play:
1. Move using WASD or the arrow keys.
2. Follow the arrow to an injured patient and press E nearby.
3. Read the patient's situation and choose an answer using 1, 2, or 3.
4. Once the decisions are correct, approach the patient and press E to treat.
5. Rescue the practice patient to finish the tutorial.
Press G at any time to open or close the tutorial controls guide. The guide pauses gameplay.
